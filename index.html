WELCOME

Building confidence in a digital world.

Artificial intelligence (AI) is becoming part of everyday life. While it offers many helpful tools and opportunities, it can also create new challenges. Understanding how AI works and recognizing potential risks can help you stay informed and safe online.

AI is technology that allows computers to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. Learn what AI is, how it is used in everyday life, and some of its benefits and drawbacks.

Recognizing Misinformation

Not everything we see online is accurate or genuine. AI can create realistic images, videos, and information that may be misleading. Learn how to question what you see and check whether information can be trusted.

03 / STAYING ALERT

Scammers are using AI to create more convincing messages, imitate voices, and make online scams harder to recognize. Learn about common warning signs and how to protect yourself from phishing and social engineering.

Understanding phishing Recognizing deepfakes Common online scams

04 / TAKING ACTION

Building Habits That Protect You

Staying safe online does not have to be complicated. Simple habits, such as slowing down, verifying information, and protecting personal details, can make a meaningful difference.

Remember these three habits:

  • Pause before clicking or responding.
  • Question unexpected messages and requests.
  • Verify information through a trusted source.
