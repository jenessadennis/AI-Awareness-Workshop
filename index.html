New to artificial intelligence? You're in the right place.

You do not need to be a technology expert to stay safe online. This website introduces important concepts in simple terms and provides practical tips you can use in everyday life.

01 · The basics

Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence, or AI, is becoming a part of everyday life. From virtual assistants to online recommendations, AI tools can help people complete tasks and find information. However, AI is not always accurate and should not be treated as a source of unquestionable truth.

What is artificial intelligence? How AI creates content How scammers use AI

02 · Question what you see

Recognizing Misinformation

Not everything we see online is accurate. AI can create realistic images, videos, audio, and written content that may be misleading. Learning to recognize misinformation and disinformation can help you make informed decisions about what you see and share.

Misinformation vs. disinformation Recognizing AI-generated images and videos Checking sources and verifying information

03 · Spot the warning signs

Spotting AI-Powered Scams

Scammers are using AI to create more convincing messages, imitate familiar voices, and personalize their attacks. Understanding common scam tactics can help you recognize suspicious messages before responding or sharing personal information.

Phishing and social engineering Fake voices, photos, and videos Common online scam scenarios

04 · Take control

Building Habits That Protect You

Staying safe online does not require advanced technical knowledge. A few simple habits can make a difference when you encounter an unexpected message, suspicious phone call, or questionable online post.

Pause before you respond Verify unexpected requests directly Protect your personal information

Remember these three habits

  • Be skeptical of unexpected requests.
  • Trust your gut when something feels wrong.
  • Verify information before taking action.
